Agent-OS privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Agent-OS is a private app used only by its maker, Tony Calamita. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it uses

When Tony signs in with Google, Agent-OS reads his YouTube playlist, and reads and changes events in his Google Calendar so they show in his day planner.

Where the data goes

The data is kept only on Tony's own computer. It is not sold, shared or sent to anyone else. It is not used for advertising.

Agent-OS's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Tony can disconnect Google in the app's Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The saved sign-in is then deleted.
